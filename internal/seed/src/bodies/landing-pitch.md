We are a small product team, and this is where we write down what we learn.
Mostly it is about the unglamorous middle of the work: what an empty screen
should say, what a review is for, where the first two hundred milliseconds of
a request go.

## What you will find here

- **Craft.** Interface decisions, argued out in public rather than settled in
  a document nobody reads twice.
- **Engineering.** Measurements before opinions, and the changes that followed
  from them.
- **Process.** The rules we hold each other to, including the ones we dropped.

Nothing here is advice. It is a record of what worked for one team, with the
numbers attached so you can tell how much to trust it.

> Write the thing down while you still remember why it was hard.

New posts land every couple of weeks. Start with the most recent, or pick a
topic and read backwards.
