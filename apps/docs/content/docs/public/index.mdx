---
title: Public packages
description: The npm packages your users install to work with your API.
---

Packages under `published/*` are versioned with Changesets and released to
npm. They are the outward-facing surface of your deployment: your users
install them to call your public API.

The client and the CLI are generated from the same oRPC contract as the
[API Reference](/openapi), so they never drift from what is deployed.
